EMPIRE STRIKES BACK FOR PARROT
CART

The Empire Strikes Back plugin
is an emulator for the
vector-based arcade sequel.

INSTALL

1. Copy ESB.CVP to /PLUGINS on
   the cart's microSD card.
2. Put your own Empire Strikes
   Back ROM files (MAME's esb
   set) in /ESB, loose or
   zipped, or as /ESB.ZIP.
3. On the cart, press B for
   Home, open Plugins, pick
   Empire Strikes Back and
   press A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: No files, since it's
  an app
Folder: /ESB
Uses: USB host
