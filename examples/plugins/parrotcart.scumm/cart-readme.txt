SCUMM FOR PARROT CART

The SCUMM plugin is an engine
for classic point-and-click
adventure games.

INSTALL

1. Copy SCUMM.CVP to /PLUGINS
   on the cart's microSD card.
2. Put your .lfl .000 files in
   /SCUMM.
3. On the cart, press B for
   Home, open Plugins, pick
   SCUMM and press A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: .lfl .000
Folder: /SCUMM
Uses: Nothing extra
