STAR WARS FOR PARROT CART

The Star Wars plugin is an
emulator for the vector-based
arcade game.

INSTALL

1. Copy STARWARS.CVP to
   /PLUGINS on the cart's
   microSD card.
2. Put your own Star Wars ROM
   files (MAME's starwars set)
   in /STARWARS, loose or
   zipped, or as
   /STARWARS.ZIP.
3. On the cart, press B for
   Home, open Plugins, pick
   Star Wars and press A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: No files, since it's
  an app
Folder: /STARWARS
Uses: USB host
