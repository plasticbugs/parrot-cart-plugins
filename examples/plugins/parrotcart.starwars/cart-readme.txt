STAR WARS FOR PARROT CART

Plays the 1983 Star Wars
arcade game from your own ROM
files, with a USB pad as the
yoke.

INSTALL

1. Copy STARWARS.CVP to
   /PLUGINS on the cart's
   microSD card.
2. Put your own Star Wars ROM
   files (MAME's starwars set)
   in /STARWARS, loose or
   zipped, or as
   /STARWARS.ZIP.
3. On the cart: Home, Plugins,
   Star Wars, then A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: No files: it is an
  app
Folder: /STARWARS
Uses: USB host
