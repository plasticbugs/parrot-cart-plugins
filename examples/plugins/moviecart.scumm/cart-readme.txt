SCUMM FOR PARROT CART

Plays SCUMM adventure games
from /SCUMM, with saves.

INSTALL

1. Copy SCUMM-0.2.7.CVP to
   /PLUGINS on the cart's
   microSD card.
2. Put your .lfl .000 files in
   /SCUMM.
3. On the cart: Home, Plugins,
   SCUMM, then A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: .lfl .000
Folder: /SCUMM
Uses: Nothing extra
