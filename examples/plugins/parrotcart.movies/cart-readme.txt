MOVIES

This plugin plays movies from
the SD card, with picture and
sound, at any length. It's
built into the Parrot Cart, so
there's nothing to install.

SETUP

A movie is a .rom file made
from a video with the movie
converter. You'll find it on
the Parrot Cart plugins site,
and it runs in your web
browser. Put your movies
anywhere on the card. Pick one
in the menu to play it.

The movie plays straight from
the card. If the card comes
out, the movie pauses. It
carries on when you put the
same card back in.

CONTROLS

- Press left fire to stop the
  movie and go back to the
  menu.
- Press RESET on the console
  to go back to the menu too.
- On a USB pad, press B or
  minus to stop the movie. B
  is the bottom face button.

USB pads need a powered hub on
the cart's USB port, because
the cart can't power them. A
pad stops the movie a moment
after you press it. If you're
already holding B when the
movie starts, let go and press
it again.

Some older movies can't be
stopped with left fire or a
pad. These are the ones made
as MegaCart files of 1 MB.
Press RESET to stop them.
