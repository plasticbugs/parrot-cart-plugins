NES FOR PARROT CART

Plays NES games from /NES,
with USB pads.

INSTALL

1. Copy NES-0.4.6.CVP to
   /PLUGINS on the cart's
   microSD card.
2. Put your .nes files in
   /NES.
3. On the cart: Home, Plugins,
   NES, then A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: .nes
Folder: /NES
Uses: USB host, PSRAM
