MASTER SYSTEM FOR PARROT CART

Plays Master System, Game Gear
and SG-1000 games, with saves.

INSTALL

1. Copy SMS.CVP to /PLUGINS on
   the cart's microSD card.
2. Put your .sms .gg .sg files
   in /SMS.
3. On the cart: Home, Plugins,
   Master System, then A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: .sms .gg .sg
Folder: /SMS
Uses: USB host, PSRAM
