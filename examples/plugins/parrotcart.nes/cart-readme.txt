NES FOR PARROT CART

The NES plugin is an emulator
for Nintendo's 8-bit console.

INSTALL

1. Copy NES.CVP to /PLUGINS on
   the cart's microSD card.
2. Put your .nes files in
   /NES.
3. On the cart, press B for
   Home, open Plugins, pick
   NES and press A.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Opens: .nes
Folder: /NES
Uses: USB host, PSRAM
